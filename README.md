☕ Coffee Shop Sales & Performance Dashboard
This repository houses the files for the Coffee Shop Sales and Performance Dashboard. This data visualization project is designed to provide quick and actionable insights to business stakeholders regarding sales trends, customer behavior, and key performance indicators (KPIs) of a coffee shop chain.

📊 Key Features & Analysis
The dashboard is built around several key analytical areas, as seen in the uploaded image:

Executive Summary: At-a-glance KPIs for Total Sales ($115.43K), Total Customer (381), Total Orders (4K), and Total Products (800%).

Sales Trends: Analysis of sales performance over time, including Total Sales by Year (2024 vs. 2025) and Total Sales by Month Name.

Weekly Performance: A line chart showing Total Sales by Week to identify peak and slow days.

Product Deep Dive: Identification of Top 5 Coffee Types by Sales, with Latte being the highest contributor.

Transaction & Customer Insights: Breakdown of sales by Payment Type (Online vs. Cash) and a visual of Customers by Discount Eligibility.

Product Sales Dashboard (2024-2025)
This repository features an interactive Product Sales Dashboard focusing on IT/Electronic product categories for the years 2024-2025. Built for sales managers, this dashboard provides critical insights into product performance, brand contribution, and team sales results.

🌟 Dashboard Highlights
Key Insights & Visualizations
Quantity by Category: A bar chart showing the quantity sold for each product category.

Monitor (344), CPU (288), and Mouse (258) are the top-selling categories by volume.

Total Sales by Brand: A detailed Pie Chart showing the revenue contribution of each brand.

The top three brands collectively account for a significant portion of total sales: Intel (17.68%), Samsung (17.12%), and Dell (13.76%).

Sales Team Performance: A slicer panel allows managers to filter the entire dashboard by individual Supervisor (e.g., Aadil Khan, Aarvi Gupta, etc.) to evaluate their team's performance.
